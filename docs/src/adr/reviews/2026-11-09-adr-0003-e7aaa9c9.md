## Alternative fairness

The alternatives are substantive and fairly bounded:

- Owner-only/deposit-based operation offers fewer interfaces but retains concentrated operational authority and less explicit retained-payer accounting.
- Replacing Owner with RBAC Admin adds API, storage, and handoff migration without improving the required capability separation.
- Historical hash exclusion/indexing changes alias policy and adds bootstrap or scan costs.
- Forced unregistration or administrative withdrawal conflicts with original-payer credit ownership.
- Historical entry rewriting increases migration work and failure surface without being necessary for this cutover.

The selected design acknowledges its own costs: ledger complexity, payer top-ups, protocol-owned backing, and trusted deployment operators.
